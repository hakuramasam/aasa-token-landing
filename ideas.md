# $AASA landing-page design directions

## Three distinct approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Chain Terminal** | A cool, precise data-console experience built around modular market facts and utilitarian typography. The emotion is trust through technical clarity. | 0.07 |
| **Authorized Mischief** | A loud digital-streetwear campaign that frames the $AASA avatar as a cultural signal rather than a generic coin. The mood is collectible, restless, and expressive. | 0.03 |
| **Quiet Treasury** | A restrained editorial finance aesthetic with generous paper space, serif headlines, and carefully framed reward mechanics. The tone is ceremonial and long-term. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Authorized Mischief

### Design Movement

**Neo-pop editorial streetwear** with pixel-art character cues, inspired by zine covers, screen-printed posters, and early digital collectible culture. It treats the supplied $AASA avatar as the lead visual asset and lets authoritative market information live within highly disciplined editorial structures.

### Core Principles

1. **Avatar first.** The supplied image is the brand’s face, always rendered large, crisply framed, and never used as an afterthought.
2. **Proof beside personality.** Electric color, playful type, and sticker-like accents are balanced by exact, plainly written mechanism cards.
3. **Momentum in layers.** A newspaper-like hierarchy, offset borders, vertical labels, and deliberate overlaps create movement without clutter.
4. **No invented market theatre.** The design makes the upcoming status obvious and avoids live price widgets, invented contract metadata, or empty promises.

### Color Philosophy

The base is **carbon black** and off-white so the supplied avatar’s saturated rainbow shirt and magenta visor remain the unmistakable visual event. A single intense **AASA Volt** lime-green accent brings urgency and acts as the ownable signal color; a controlled hot-magenta secondary accent echoes the avatar’s eyewear. Color is used as a compositional stroke, not as a decorative gradient.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is assembled as an asymmetric **street-poster wall**. The hero uses a large art block on the left and a narrow information spine on the right. Subsequent sections become staggered editorial panels, with vertical rails, overprinted labels, and strongly aligned baselines instead of a conventional centered card grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A lime **“AASA SIGNAL”** ticker rule that threads through headings and section dividers.
2. Offset black registration marks and minimal halftone grain as a visual reference to printed posters.
3. Tiny boxed **B20 / META / BASE** indicators that create a repeated systems language.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions feel tactile and intentional. Buttons invert the high-contrast color system; fact panels lift a few pixels and reveal a crisp lime rule. Navigation anchors scroll directly to the relevant evidence, never to a generic marketing destination. The cited source link is treated as a verifiable artifact.

### Animation

All motion uses fast, deliberate transforms and opacity changes with a snappy custom ease. On first view, the avatar, hero headline, and provenance strip enter on offset timing; the ticker moves continuously but pauses for reduced-motion preferences. Card hovers use a subtle two-pixel translation and sharp shadow change rather than elastic or floating effects. All animated sequences respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** delivers compact, forceful navigation and body information. **Archivo Black** provides the poster-grade display voice for titles and token symbols. Headlines are uppercased at broad tracking with dense line-height; technical labels use Space Grotesk in all caps and smaller tracking for disciplined contrast. No Inter is used.

### Brand Essence

**$AASA is an upcoming Base-native B20 that turns the Authorized Agentic Social Avatar into a stakeable path toward the META-powered AI-owned NFT launchpad ecosystem.**

Personality: **audacious, native, intentional**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, declarative, and culture-forward. CTAs are evidentiary and directional; microcopy favors plain mechanism language over hype.

> “AVATAR AUTHORIZED. REWARDS ROUTED.”

> “Read the launch signal — then verify the route.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is an oversized **$AASA** set in Archivo Black with an offset lime registration shadow and a small three-square pixel glyph. The official supplied avatar image is the token artwork and should appear in the hero, source card, header mark, and favicon-adjacent brand stamp. No replacement AI-generated logo will be substituted for the supplied artwork.

### Signature Brand Color

**AASA Volt — #B8FF1D.**

## Style Decisions

- The supplied animated $AASA avatar artwork is the official token visual and will be used as the primary hero art.
- The landing page will position $AASA as preparing to launch and will link the supplied X announcement as its supporting public source.
- Website copy will explain $AASA/META staking and fee flow as described in the announcement, without price, yield, address, or timing claims.
- **Color rule accepted:** AASA Volt is the primary color for actions, proof emphasis, tickers, and key numerals. Hot magenta is the cultural counterpoint; cyan and orange remain sparse avatar-derived accents.
- **Wordmark rule accepted:** $AASA carries the heavy poster wordmark treatment, a lime registration offset, and the three-square pixel glyph wherever the brand is stamped.
- **Layout rule accepted:** Every major section incorporates a street-poster device such as a vertical rail, offset field, overprinted label, registration mark, or deliberate overlap.
