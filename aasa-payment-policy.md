# $AASA Creator Payment and Reward Policy

## Purpose and boundary

This policy governs a **draft-only** preparation record for $AASA NFT creation fees. It does not authorize a payment, token approval, conversion, reward-token purchase, staking transaction, or claim. A real payment path remains blocked until the project publishes verified Base Safe addresses, an audited contract set, and explicit governance approval.

## Objective programme-rating model

The fee tier is determined by a fixed set of documented delivery checks. It must **not** use NFT floor price, token price, volume, trading activity, or any promise of return. A creator can only request a tier after satisfying every listed criterion; the selected tier and criteria version are recorded in the payment plan.

| Tier | Fee | Monthly reserve | Measurable criteria |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| R1 — Standard | 10,000 $AASA | 2,000 $AASA | Creator account verified; artwork, title, description, and recipient wallet supplied; no custom staking programme. |
| R2 — Curated | 25,000 $AASA | 5,000 $AASA | R1 checks plus collection statement, rights acknowledgement, and one verified reward-token allowlist entry. |
| R3 — Staking-ready | 100,000 $AASA | 20,000 $AASA | R2 checks plus published monthly eligibility method, reward-flow description, and independently reviewed staking integration. |
| R4 — Ecosystem | 500,000 $AASA | 100,000 $AASA | R3 checks plus an approved ecosystem integration, named operations owner, published budget policy, and monthly reporting commitment. |
| R5 — Protocol partner | 1,000,000 $AASA | 200,000 $AASA | R4 checks plus 2-of-3 treasury approval, independent smart-contract review, incident response contact, and a public risk disclosure. |

The reserve is a fixed **20%** of the selected $AASA fee. The initial approved reward-token record is `$BPAD` at `0xf5F11BC9Be9D6690f795D04d2fc9bdd097008a2B`; additional stock-token tickers cannot enter the allowlist without an exact verified Base address.

## Treasury guardrails

| Guardrail | Draft-only policy |
|---|---|
| Custody | Two Base Safe accounts: `AASA Treasury` and `AASA Rewards`; neither address is configured yet. |
| Authorization | 2-of-3 Safe confirmations are required for any future treasury proposal. |
| Cadence | One funding/distribution preparation record per monthly epoch. |
| Asset allowlist | `$BPAD` only until additional Base reward-token addresses are verified and approved. |
| Conversion constraints | Any future proposal must cap maximum slippage at **200 bps** and expire after **72 hours**. |
| Settlement evidence | A future B20 payment must carry a unique memo/reference and a successful on-chain settlement proof before a reward record advances beyond preparation. |
| Fail-closed status | Until vault addresses and contracts are verified, every plan stays `preparation_only` and `treasury_setup_required`. |

## Review and change control

Changes to fee, criteria, reward allocation, token allowlist, or treasury constraints require a new policy version. Existing payment plans retain the exact criteria and guardrails that were in force when they were prepared.
